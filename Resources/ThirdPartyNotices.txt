This distribution includes an unmodified official KDE Connect macOS app.
KDE Connect and its dependencies remain separate upstream components. Their
copyright and license files are in:
    Contents/Helpers/KDE Connect.app/Contents/Resources/licenses

KDE Connect binary: 26.08.2 (Apple Silicon)
https://download.kde.org/stable/release-service/26.08.2/macos/kdeconnect-kde-macos-clang-arm64.dmg
KDE Connect license texts are also in Contents/Resources/UpstreamLicenses.
The matching source archive is supplied beside the ZIP in GitHub Releases.
KDE Connect corresponding source and license files:
https://download.kde.org/stable/release-service/26.08.2/src/kdeconnect-kde-26.08.2.tar.xz
Upstream project and build/dependency instructions:
https://invent.kde.org/network/kdeconnect-kde
https://invent.kde.org/packaging/craft-blueprints-kde

KDE Connect is not built or modified by this project's CI. The full upstream
app, including its dependency libraries and license notices, is preserved.
This native front end is not an official KDE product.
